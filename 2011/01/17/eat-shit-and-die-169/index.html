Eat Shit And Die 169

by bpatrick on January 17, 2011 at 12:00 am
  • Eat Shit and Die

Emulsifier to the rescue!! The previous/next buttons are new and improved! I’m going to work on more consistency in the update schedule. Look for comics on Mondays but I’m still going to put them up as soon as possible — I’m not going to wait ’til Monday – I just want to be at least weekly with updates. It’s a goal.

Oh, and if you get a chance to check it out Cracked.com’s new book has an illustration by me! It was pretty cool to go into a Barnes & Noble and see a book on the shelf that I was “in”. I always liked Cracked as a kid – probably because of John Severin whose line work I’m still in awe of.
