eat shit & die 187

by bpatrick on June 15, 2011 at 12:00 am
  • Eat Shit and Die

Not really a laugh riot this week. Thanks for being patient (especially you, cbobgo). It’s pretty bad when you feel too shitty to make comics about how shitty you feel. I have a huge backlog of ideas but I wanted to do the one that would be everyone’s new favorite and then realized none of them will be and that I had just wasted time. So I decided I’m just going to make them – shitty or not. So here’s one sure to disappoint but I like Allison’s haircut.
