By the time I finished I was ready to put my head throught the screen. I added a link to my sketch blog if anyone wants to check that out. I also added some new links to comics I enjoy. I hope to add many more artists to the list when I have a minute. One can also look at my deviant art gallery for alot of the comics you find here plus some stuff you don’t
