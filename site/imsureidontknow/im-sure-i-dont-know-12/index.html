    Surprisingly great. I’m also opposed to all things scatological in general, but you make an extremely good point about art and its appreciation. It’s unclear (in a good way) whether the flashbacks are for our eyes only or if the characters are actually conscious of these – if they are conscious they really need to learn some self-awareness. To not appreciate art because you don’t like the medium, the point, or what have you is horribly closed-minded and we should appreciate words that are deeply expressive of whatever, however. But of course there’s a degree of alienation inherent in that, and I wish those who consider themselves artists would give more respect to their fellow artists’ expression (even if it is literally shit). Nicely irreverent way of making a good point.
